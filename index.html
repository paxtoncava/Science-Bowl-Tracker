Leaderboard

Sorted by Pts / 10 TU: tossup points per 10 tossups heard while seated (+4 correct, −4 neg), so playing time doesn't matter. Click any column to re-sort. Gray rows have fewer than tossups heard, so treat them as early estimates. Bench is written answers while sitting out.

Category strengths

Each cell is the chance the player knows a tossup in that category, from seated correct answers plus bench written answers. Small samples are pulled toward the player's overall rate so one lucky question doesn't dominate. The small number is known / heard.

Team Builder

A team scores a tossup if anyone on it knows the answer, so the best team isn't just the top 4 on the leaderboard; it's the 4 who cover each other's gaps. Coverage = estimated chance at least one starter knows a tossup, averaged over the six categories. The alternate is whoever adds the most on top of the starters. Tap a name to lock them in (e.g. a captain), tap again to exclude them.
